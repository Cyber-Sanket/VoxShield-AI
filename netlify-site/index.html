SIGNAL PROCESSING DEEP DIVE

Acoustic Indicators of Voice Synthesis

Neural speech generators frequently leave subtle mathematical signatures across spectral and cepstral domains. Here is how VoxShield AI analyzes each descriptor.

CEPSTRAL DOMAIN

MFCC (Mel-Frequency Cepstral Coefficients)

Physical Role: Captures the vocal tract envelope and acoustic timbre.

Forensic Relevance: Human speech exhibits smooth formant resonances. Vocoders (HiFi-GAN) exhibit unnatural discontinuities in higher-order coefficients (MFCC 12–20).

SPECTRAL DOMAIN

Log Mel-Spectrogram (128 Bands)

Physical Role: Perceptual time-frequency energy distribution across pitch bins.

Forensic Relevance: Deepfakes frequently show checkerboard artifacts, blurred harmonic striations, or sudden brick-wall cutoffs above 4 kHz.

SPECTRAL TRAJECTORY

Spectral Centroid & Bandwidth

Physical Role: Center of spectral mass ("sound brightness") and frequency spread.

Forensic Relevance: Cloned audio often shows depressed spectral centroids due to internal model downsampling or erratic spikes caused by synthetic high-frequency hiss.

HIGH-FREQUENCY CUTOFF

Spectral Rolloff (85% Energy Boundary)

Physical Role: Frequency threshold below which 85% of total signal energy lies.

Forensic Relevance: Synthetic voices trained on lower-rate audio (16 kHz / 24 kHz) show sharp rolloff drop-offs compared to full-bandwidth studio recordings.

TEMPORAL DYNAMICS

Zero Crossing Rate (ZCR)

Physical Role: Frequency of waveform sign-changes per second.

Forensic Relevance: Natural unvoiced speech (fricatives like 's', 'f', 'sh') creates rich ZCR spikes. Synthetic audio models often blur these transitions.

ENERGY & PHASE

RMS Energy & Spectral Flatness

Physical Role: Loudness envelope dynamics and measure of tonality vs noisiness.

Forensic Relevance: Natural speakers introduce micro-tremors, breathing pauses, and natural decays. Cloned voices exhibit overly uniform energy and phase buzz.

Strict Academic Integrity Architecture

As an academic cybersecurity platform, VoxShield AI enforces a non-negotiable principle: Never fabricate predictions, accuracy figures, or synthetic confidence percentages.

MODE A

Model-Based Detection

Operates exclusively when trained model weights exist in the models/ directory (Scikit-Learn .joblib or PyTorch .pt).

  • ✓ Reads model weights from configurable directory
  • ✓ Returns genuine classification: Real, Fake, or Inconclusive
  • ✓ Displays authentic model confidence derived from decision boundary
  • ✓ Audited training script: tools/train_baseline.py
MODE B

Demo / Feature Analysis Mode

Automatically activates when no trained model is loaded. Fully computes all acoustic features without fabricating automated predictions.

  • ✓ Full signal conditioning & feature extraction active
  • ✓ Explicitly labeled: "Analysis Only - Prediction Unavailable"
  • ✓ Confidence strictly set to None (zero fake numbers)
  • ✓ Informs analysts that verified datasets are required for classification
